\begin{abstract}
We build a handheld pendulum device as a means for participants to feel and manipulate the timing of musical playback (in our case, played by a robotic glockenspiel). The physical properties of the pendulum make this interaction scheme steady and intuitive, catering to varied levels of expertise, and particularly suitable as an introductory, educational means of musical engagement. Social play is also encouraged through a multi-player cooperative mode. We envisage that similar physically-guided, tactile interaction schemes can be employed in wider contexts and with different media formats to foster participant exploration, discovery, and creativity.
\end{abstract}
